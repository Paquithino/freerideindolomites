How to read the map

Blue and purple = snow, pink = snow mixed with rain, green, yellow and red = rain; the more intense the colour, the heavier the precipitation (mm of water per hour). The dashed white line is the snow line: on one side it snows, on the other it rains.

The map uses the ICON-2I high-resolution model by ItaliaMeteo-ARPAE (fallbacks: ICON-D2 and ECMWF) on a grid of about 10 km, then smoothed: individual valleys aren't visible and local phenomena may be missed. For a spot, open its page: there you'll find the FreeRide forecast with several models and snow by altitude.
