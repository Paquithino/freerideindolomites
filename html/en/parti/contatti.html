Who built FreeRide in Dolomites

Hi, I'm Francesco Fabbro, a software engineer and freerider. FreeRide in Dolomites was conceived, designed and built entirely by me: the idea, the design, the code, the texts, the model calibration method and the automatic data collection.

I built it to have in one place, even on the phone, everything you need to choose where and when to find the best snow in the Dolomites and Friuli, without jumping between ten different sites.
